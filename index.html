Why We Build

Our take on the future

Good lives will take shape through casual learning

Good lives have always been shaped by good planning and execution. When execution was slow, mistakes in decision-making had fewer chances to compound. AI changes that balance, making casual learning essential to living well.

01 AI makes decision-making the bottleneck.

As execution gets extremely faster and cheaper, the slowest and most expensive part becomes humans making decisions.

02 Decision quality depends on reflexive intuition.

As decision time gets shorter and more fragmented to keep up with execution speed, the quality of reflexive intuition becomes key.

03 Reflexive intuition grows from high-quality repetitive drills.

Cultivating the right intuition to a reflexive level requires highly curated repetitive practice, like pianists training with scales.

04 We build casual drill apps to train intuition for better decisions.

Drills need to be casual and readily approachable so they can be woven into ordinary life and sustain repetition.
